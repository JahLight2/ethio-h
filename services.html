03 — How a visit runs

Nothingimprovised.

The same four steps on every visit, in the same order, recorded on the same sheet.

01

Arrive

A uniformed team at the agreed hour, with its own water, cloths, blades and finishing kit. Nothing is borrowed from the house.

02

Work in order

Top to bottom, dry before wet, stone before glass. The route is the one written on your scope page.

03

Check in the light

Every glass and brass surface is inspected against the light you will see it in — not the light we worked in.

04

Hand over

We walk the rooms with you and correct anything on the spot. The sheet is signed and kept for the next visit.
